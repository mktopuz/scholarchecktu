What GitHub Pages can and cannot do

Can: host this demo, the vendor screens, and the register screens for free, on any phone, with no app-store install.

Cannot: share one live balance across many phones. That needs a small backend + database (for example Supabase/Firebase, or a tiny server). The vendor flow, QR format, and ledger in this demo are designed so that backend can be added later without changing how vendors work: same QR code, same amount + scan + confirm steps, but balances checked on the server instead of in the browser.

Not in this version, by your decision: no payment processor, no online top-up, no vendor payouts. Money is taken only at the register, and vendor totals here are a report — payouts, if any, happen outside the app.

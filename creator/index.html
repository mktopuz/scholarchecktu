Scholarly Creator

Build your thesis, dissertation, or journal paper for Overleaf — no LaTeX experience needed.

Private by design. Everything you type stays in this browser tab. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — the finished project is a .zip file you download and open in Overleaf yourself.

1 What are you writing?

Pick the kind of document first — the template choices follow from it. You can change this later; nothing you type is lost.

Template

New to Overleaf? Overleaf is a free online editor for LaTeX documents — the typesetting system most universities and journals use for theses and papers. You don't write code here: fill in the boxes, and this app writes the LaTeX for you. At the end you get a .zip to open in Overleaf, where you press one button to build your PDF.

4 Chapters / sections

Write in plain text. A blank line starts a new paragraph. bold and italic markup work, and $...$ passes through as math. To cite a reference, type \cite{key} with the key you give it in step 6.

5 Figures, tables & equations

Figures

Upload an image and it travels inside your .zip — Overleaf will show it in your document.

Tables

Equations

Type LaTeX math (without the $ signs) or tap a snippet to insert it. Equations are numbered automatically.

6 References

Each reference gets a short key (like smith2024) that you cite in your text with \cite{smith2024}. The app writes the bibliography file for you.

7 Review & download


    

      
      
    

    
Open it in Overleaf (no account yet? start here)

    
    
      
  1. Create a free Overleaf account (one time only). Click Create a free Overleaf account and sign up with your email address, or with Google if you prefer. The free plan is all you need — no credit card is asked for. You only do this once; every project after this opens the same way.
    2. 
      
  2. Download your .zip from this page using the button above.
    3. 
      
  3. Upload it to Overleaf. In Overleaf, click New Project → Upload Project and choose the .zip file you just downloaded. Your project opens with everything inside — there is nothing else to upload.
    4. 
      
  4. Press Recompile and wait a few seconds. Your PDF appears on the right side of the screen.
    5. 
      
  5. Get your PDF. Click the download icon above the preview (or Menu → Download PDF). To keep editing, just change the text on the left and press Recompile again.
    6. 
    

    
If Overleaf shows an error on the first compile, press Recompile once more — references and page numbers settle on the second pass.
